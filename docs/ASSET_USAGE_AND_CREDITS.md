# Asset Usage and Credits

## Shipping policy

Only credited source assets from the supplied `HellRPG-LPC-GameReady-Core-v0.2` archive are used. The user-generated generator exports under `90_user_generated_PROTOTYPE_ONLY_credits_missing` are not copied into this project and are not presented as production-cleared assets.

All selected PNG files in `dist/assets/` are unchanged copies. Runtime scaling and frame selection happen in Phaser. The original attribution records are preserved under `dist/assets/licenses/`.

The supplied records credit, among others, Eliza Wyatt (DeathsDarling), Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Hyptosis, Stephen Challener (Redshrike), Demetrius, Johannes Sjölund, William.Thompsonj and the authors listed in the preserved helmet/ULPC records. This note is an organization record, not legal advice; re-check final store-page attribution before commercial release.

## Excluded material

- No Dekaron/2Moons art, names, maps, characters, monsters, UI or story material is included.
- No prototype-only LPC generator export with missing per-layer credits is included.
- Mixed-license helmet families not already approved by the supplied core archive are not used.
- No source spritesheet was recolored or destructively edited for this build.
